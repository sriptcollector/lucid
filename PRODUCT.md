# Product

## Register

product

## Users

- **Orion (owner, daily power user).** Records meetings/ideas on a Plaud device; reviews notes on his phone (via Cloudflare tunnel, often one-handed, in transit) and on desktop. Wants the takeaway from a recording in seconds, not the raw transcript.
- **Friends (self-hosters).** Non-technical people who download `Lucid.exe`, run the setup wizard, and use it for their own recordings. First-run success without help is the bar.
- **Edge users who must not be excluded:** small/cheap phones, bright sunlight (dark theme contrast matters), slow tunnel connections, screen-reader and keyboard users, people overwhelmed by dense transcripts (cognitive load).

## Product Purpose

Self-hosted AI notetaker for Plaud voice recordings: auto-pulls from Plaud cloud, transcribes, diarizes (voice ID), and analyzes each recording into a clean note — summary, headline, people, action items, ideas — plus a People directory/relationship tracker, Projects, a CRM board, and Journal/Today/Review views. Success = **time-to-insight**: after a recording syncs, the user finds and acts on what matters (summary → action items → people) in under a minute, and a friend completes first-run setup unaided. Success is NOT engagement or time-in-app.

## Brand Personality

Calm, precise, private. A quiet instrument you trust with your whole life's audio — premium flat monochrome, no gamification, no noise. Feels like a well-made tool (Linear/Things energy), not a social product.

## Anti-references

- Notion-like sprawling flexibility; this is a focused pipeline, not a workspace.
- SaaS dashboard clichés: hero metrics, gradient accents, identical card grids.
- Engagement-bait patterns: streaks, badges, infinite feeds, red notification dots.
- Generic AI-tool gloss (purple gradients, sparkle icons everywhere).

## Design Principles

1. **The note is the product.** Every screen exists to get someone to the takeaway of a recording faster; anything that delays that earns its place or dies.
2. **Calm density.** Dense information, quiet presentation — brightness and weight carry hierarchy in a single monochrome ramp; the one near-white accent is reserved for the primary action.
3. **Never lose a recording's trust.** States are honest: syncing, transcribing, analyzing, failed — always visible, never faked.
4. **One-handed phone first.** Orion reads on a phone through a tunnel; primary actions live in thumb reach and survive slow networks.
5. **Consistency over surprise.** One component vocabulary across Today/Journal/People/Projects/CRM; delight lives in micro-moments, not layouts.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG AA: body text ≥4.5:1 on the dark surfaces (the gray ink ramp must be checked, not assumed), touch targets ≥44px, visible focus order, reduced-motion alternatives.
- Voice-first users may have motor limitations — keyboard access and large targets are not optional.
- Cognitive accessibility: summaries before transcripts, progressive disclosure of detail.
